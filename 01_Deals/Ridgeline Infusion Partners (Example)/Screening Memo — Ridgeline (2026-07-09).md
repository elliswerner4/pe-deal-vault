## 3-5 questions for the founder

1. Revenue and gross profit by payer, top 5 — and by J-code, top 10. (Moves the bid range more than any other single item.)

## What would make us wrong

The thesis assumes site-of-care steering keeps favoring ambulatory/home settings. A payer pivot to white-bagging (dispensing through specialty pharmacy, cutting the provider out of drug margin) would compress the drug-spread portion of gross profit and could not be offset by administration fees alone. Probability unquantified at screen; the payer-contract review at first-round stage tests it.
