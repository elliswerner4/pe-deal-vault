# Mode: Diligence & Exclusivity

**Trigger.** LOI signed; 30-60 days of exclusivity.

**Deliverable.** Final IC Memo (~30-35 pages plus appendices, structure: [[Memo — Final IC]]) + bid letter.

**The 10 diligence workstreams** the analyst coordinates and synthesizes:

1. **Quality of Earnings (QofE)** — EBITDA bridge with defensibility tags, revenue quality, working-capital normalization, net-debt definition, capex split.
2. **Commercial DD** — TAM/SAM/SOM (top-down + bottom-up), competitive positioning, customer-voice synthesis, NRR/cohorts, pricing power, win/loss.
3. **Legal DD** — top-10 customer + supplier contracts, change-of-control / anti-assignment risk, IP chain-of-title, litigation, regulatory, antitrust.
4. **Tax** — federal/state/local exposures, sales-and-use post-Wayfair, NOL limits, structuring (asset vs. stock vs. 338(h)(10) vs. F-reorg).
5. **Management / background** — references, background checks, assessment, comp benchmarking, MIP / rollover design.
6. **Tech / cyber / data** — architecture, scalability, security (SOC 2 Type II baseline), OSS license risk, breach history, privacy.
7. **ESG** — GHG inventory, climate risk, EDCI schema, ILPA reporting alignment.
8. **Insurance (incl. R&W)** — coverage adequacy, tail coverage, R&W binding economics, tax indemnity.
9. **HR** — comp and benefits, key-person retention, restrictive covenants (state-by-state), culture/engagement signals.
10. **Environmental** — Phase I ESA (ASTM E1527-21), Phase II if triggered, PFAS exposure, permits.

**the analyst's job across workstreams:** coordinate coverage (no gaps, no duplication), reconcile findings across workstreams (e.g., legal flagged a contract QofE counted as recurring — surface the contradiction), integrate everything into the EBITDA bridge / sources & uses / risk matrix, and surface retrade signals (QofE-adjusted EBITDA materially below CIM EBITDA is a renegotiation lever).

**Output shape.** Final IC memo with all 8 killer slides; appendix with 1-2 page workstream summaries.
