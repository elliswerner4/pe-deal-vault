# The 8-Question Framework

The analytical spine of every deal, at every depth. Each question maps to a section of the IC memo. Screening answers them in a sentence each; a re-underwrite grades each against the original thesis with citations.

| # | Question | What it tests | Maps to memo section |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | What is the primary customer value proposition? | Is the core "why this exists" true? | Business overview |
| 2 | What are the most important competitive characteristics? | Is the moat intact? | Market / competitive |
| 3 | Why do customers buy from them? | Demand durability, switching costs | Customers / commercial |
| 4 | What are the 2-3 most logical growth vectors? | Is there a credible forward thesis? | Growth / value-creation plan |
| 5 | Why hasn't someone destroyed them? | Defensibility, structural protection | Competitive / risk |
| 6 | What would kill this business? | Downside, tail risk | Risk factors |
| 7 | What would double EBITDA? | The value-creation math | Value creation / returns |
| 8 | Should we own it (at this price)? | The verdict | Recommendation |

## Q8 adapts by mode

- **Fresh underwriting:** buy / pass at this entry multiple
- **[[Mode — Re-Underwriting|Re-underwriting]] / CV:** hold / sell / CV / refinance at this price and structure
- **[[Mode — Quarterly Monitoring|Quarterly review]]:** are we still on track to the original Q8 outcome?

## Grading scale (re-underwriting)

Each question's original thesis claim is graded against current reality: **strengthened / held / weakened / broken** — with the variance explained, evidence cited, and confidence stated. This is variance analysis, not re-narration: find what the original thesis claimed, determine what is true today, and decompose the gap.

Answer structure per question: original thesis claim (cited) → current reality (cited) → variance grade + one-paragraph why → 2-5 sector KPIs ([[_Sector Index]]) → 3-7 load-bearing facts → red flags fired ([[Red-Flag Triggers]]) → confidence → open questions.
