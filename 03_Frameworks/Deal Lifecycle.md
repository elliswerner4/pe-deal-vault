# Deal Lifecycle

Where a deal sits in its life determines what you produce and how deep you go. Match the output to the stage — screening depth on a final-bid deal is malpractice in both directions.

| Stage | Artifact produced | Length / form |
|---|---|---|
| Sourcing / origination | Pipeline log entry, opportunity snapshot | 1-page snapshot |
| CIM screen | Screening memo with thesis + IOI recommendation | 1-2 pages |
| First-round process | First-round IC memo, working LBO sketch | ~15 pages |
| Management meeting | Mgmt-meeting question pack, debrief memo | 2 pages + Q pack |
| Second-round diligence | Workstream summaries, refined LBO with sensitivities | 20-30 pages cumulative |
| LOI / final bid | Final IC memo, bid letter | 30-35 pages + bid letter |
| Closing / 100-day | Thesis-to-KPI mapping, board prep | 1-page mapping per KPI |
| Quarterly monitoring | Variance-vs-thesis report | 3-5 pages per portco |
| Re-underwriting / exit / CV | Re-underwriting memo, buyer-universe map | 15-25 pages |

## The five operating modes

- [[Mode — Screening]] — inbound CIM, banker outreach, or a cold name
- [[Mode — First-Round IC]] — IOI submission in ~4 weeks
- [[Mode — Diligence & Exclusivity]] — LOI signed; the 10 workstreams
- [[Mode — Re-Underwriting]] — hold / sell / CV / refinance decision
- [[Mode — Quarterly Monitoring]] — variance-vs-thesis, every quarter

## Cross-mode consistency

Regardless of mode:
- Every numeric claim has a citation or a shown derivation
- The 8-question framework is the analytical spine (depth varies by mode)
- Killer-slide discipline applies (slides 1-3 for screening, 1-5 for first-round, all 8 for final IC and re-underwriting)
- Confidence tags on non-trivial claims
- Honest pre-mortem ("what would make us wrong") in every memo

Modes inherit from each other. A first-round IC memo expands into a final IC memo; a final IC memo seeds the eventual re-underwriting; quarterly variance reports compound into the re-underwriting bridge. **The same analytical spine carries the deal across its whole life.**
