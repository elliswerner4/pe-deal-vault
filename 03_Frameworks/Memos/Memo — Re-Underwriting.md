# RE-UNDERWRITING MEMO (15-25 pages)

Opens with the [[House Style]] header; closes with its footer and blank sign-off lines. Voice rules and killer-slide checklist: [[Voice & Killer Slides]].

For existing portcos at a hold / sell / CV / refinance decision point.

1. **Executive Summary & Recommendation** (1 page) — single-paragraph thesis-vs-actuals verdict (held / weakened / broken) and hold/sell/CV/refinance recommendation. For CV: also state the proposed CV strike, the LP roll-vs-cash-elect economics in one line, and whether the recommendation is to support the proposal or push back.
2. **Deal Background & Current State** — original investment date, fund, lead partner, board members, hold period to date, current mark, prior recap/refi events.
3. **Business Overview**
4. **Customers & Suppliers** — variance from underwriting case; top-N concentration trajectory.
5. **Market & Competition** — how the market moved vs. underwrite; named competitive moves since.
6. **Management & Organization** — departures since underwrite; current backable-ness assessment.
7. **Growth / Value-Creation Plan — Progress vs. Underwrite** — original initiatives → status today → quantified delta (delivered / partial / missed / not yet pursued).
8. **Historical Financials** — revenue / EBITDA / margin trajectory vs. underwrite case; mark history.
9. **Thesis-vs-Actuals Bridge** — the heart of the re-underwrite. Decompose variance into: price, volume, mix, M&A, one-offs, market-environment shift.
10. **Returns Analysis at Current Exit Assumption** — implied MOIC/IRR vs. underwrite; base/bull/bear at current expected exit timing. All math shown or computed in the analysis tool.
11. **CV-Specific Module** (where applicable — the deepest part of this memo type):
    - **Fairness analysis** — proposed CV strike vs. independent value triangulation (DCF, comp triangulation, recent secondary marks)
    - **NAV mark vs. CV strike spread** — quantify the delta and each side's methodology
    - **LP economics** — roll vs. cash-elect math for each LP class (selling LP, rolling LP, new buyer, GP)
    - **Conflicts map** — every place GP incentives diverge from each LP class
    - **Fee & carry reset analysis** — the new management-fee stream and crystallized/reset carry the CV creates for the GP; whether rolling LPs face adverse economic change vs. the ILPA status-quo standard
    - **GP alignment check** — is the GP rolling 100% of crystallized carry? Fresh GP commitment?
    - **ILPA governance checklist** — election window (≥30 days), information symmetry (existing LPs get the same information given to buyers), LPAC process, status-quo option
    - **Fairness opinion provider & scope** — who, and what assumptions they are underwriting
    - **Litigation-risk pattern check** — flag any process or disclosure pattern resembling known GP-led-secondary enforcement fact patterns (information asymmetry, fairness opinion built on GP-supplied unverified assumptions, improper carry crystallization)
12. **Buyer-Universe Map** (where a sale path is under consideration) — strategic + financial, with rationale per candidate.
13. **Risk Factors** (re-graded since underwrite).
14. **The Eight Questions** — each graded against original thesis with citations.
15. **Recommendation & Decision Memo** — hold / sell / CV / refinance; conditions that change it.
16. **Open-Question List** — explicit residual diligence gaps, including every logged `[Limitation]`.
17. **Sign-off Lines** — mandatory blank human-signature lines; CV valuation conclusions explicitly require human IC sign-off.
