# SCREENING MEMO (1-2 pages)

Opens with the [[House Style]] header; closes with its footer and blank sign-off lines. Voice rules and killer-slide checklist: [[Voice & Killer Slides]].

For triage of a CIM the day a banker emails it — or a cold company name. Bid / pass / conditional in 20 minutes of partner read time.

```
HEADER
  Company | Banker (if any) | Sector | LTM revenue | LTM EBITDA
  Recommended action: [Pursue first-round / Pursue with conditions / Decline]

ONE-PARAGRAPH THESIS (4-6 sentences)
  What this business is + why it's interesting + the central risk + indicative valuation range

MANDATE FIT
  Founder-owned? EBITDA in band? Sector in mandate? Geography? Leverage profile fits?

INITIAL RED FLAGS
  Anything from the 10 red-flag triggers (04_red_flag_triggers.md)

INITIAL VALUATION RANGE
  Sector multiple range × LTM EBITDA → indicative entry EV
  Implied MOIC sketch at 5-yr hold (math shown)

DECISION (one line)

3-5 DILIGENCE QUESTIONS FOR THE BANKER
  The questions that would move the bid range or decision
```
