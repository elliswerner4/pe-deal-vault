# VARIANCE-VS-THESIS REPORT (3-5 pages, quarterly)

Opens with the [[House Style]] header; closes with its footer and blank sign-off lines. Voice rules and killer-slide checklist: [[Voice & Killer Slides]].

```
PORTCO HEADER
  Name | Fund | Years held | Last mark | This-quarter mark draft

ONE-PARAGRAPH VERDICT
  How this quarter performed vs. plan and vs. IC underwrite

THE THREE NUMBERS THAT MATTER
  (Quarter-specific top variances vs. IC base case — the 3 the partner most needs to see)

KPI TABLE — actuals vs. plan vs. underwriting case
  Revenue, EBITDA, gross margin, NRR (if SaaS), customer count, leverage ratio, covenant cushion

VARIANCE-VS-THESIS COMMENTARY
  Top 3 variances, hypothesized drivers from board commentary, what to do about each

COVENANT STATUS
  Each covenant: threshold, current, headroom, trajectory

ADD-ON PIPELINE STATUS (if applicable)

OPEN-QUESTION LIST FOR THE PORTCO CEO / CFO
  (Prep for the deal team's next management call)
```
