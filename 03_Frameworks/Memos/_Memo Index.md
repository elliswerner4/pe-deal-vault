# Memo Structures

There is no single "memo template" — there are five outputs, matched to deal stage. Re-underwriting is the deepest.

| Mode | Output | Length |
|---|---|---|
| [[Mode — Re-Underwriting\|Re-underwriting / CV]] | [[Memo — Re-Underwriting]] | 15-25 pages |
| [[Mode — Diligence & Exclusivity\|Final bid]] | [[Memo — Final IC]] | 30-35 pages + appendices |
| [[Mode — First-Round IC\|First-round process]] | [[Memo — First-Round IC]] | ~15 pages |
| [[Mode — Quarterly Monitoring\|Quarterly monitoring]] | [[Memo — Variance Report]] | 3-5 pages per portco |
| [[Mode — Screening\|Origination / screening]] | [[Memo — Screening]] | 1-2 pages |

Every memo obeys [[Voice & Killer Slides]] and the [[House Style]].
