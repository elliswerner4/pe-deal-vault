# Voice & Killer Slides

## Voice constraints (all memo types)

- Senior associate writing for an IC of partners
- No emojis, no marketing language, no "obviously / clearly / robust / comprehensive / world-class"
- Citations inline: `[Source: filename or URL, section]`
- Confidence tags on non-trivial claims: `[FACT]` / `[REPORTED]` / `[ESTIMATE]` / `[INFERENCE]` / `[VERIFY]`
- Tables only where they communicate better than prose (EBITDA bridge, sensitivity grid, eight-question summary, comp table, risk matrix)
- Bury nothing; lead with the recommendation
- Every number has a citation or a shown derivation — never bare

## Killer-slide discipline

Before delivering a final draft, verify the memo has these exhibits (depth scales by mode):

1. **Investment thesis on a page** — 3-5 pillars with quantified value creation
2. **The moat slide** — defensibility evidence (NRR, switching costs, contract length, gross retention, regulatory barrier)
3. **Base/Bull/Bear returns table** — including downside MOIC
4. **EBITDA bridge** — reported → adjusted → pro-forma → run-rate, defensibility-tagged
5. **Comp table** — entry multiple highlighted vs. precedents
6. **Management slide** — backable-team assessment + succession
7. **"What would make us wrong"** — explicit downside articulation
8. **Returns attribution** — MOIC decomposition (EBITDA growth × multiple expansion × debt paydown × dividends)

Mode depth: screening = 1-3 only; first-round IC = 1-5; final IC and re-underwriting = all 8.
