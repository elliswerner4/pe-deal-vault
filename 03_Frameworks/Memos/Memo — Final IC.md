# FINAL IC MEMO (30-35 pages + appendices)

Opens with the [[House Style]] header; closes with its footer and blank sign-off lines. Voice rules and killer-slide checklist: [[Voice & Killer Slides]].

For final bid approval and LOI/SPA negotiation mandate.

1. **Executive Summary & Recommendation** (1 page — must stand alone) — deal header, thesis verdict, recommendation, top 3 risks, top 3 conditions.
2. **Deal Background & Process** — banker, process dynamics, exclusivity timeline.
3. **Business Overview** (1-2 pages).
4. **Customers & Suppliers** — concentration, churn, NRR, contract terms.
5. **Market & Competition** — TAM update post-CDD, named competitors, share data, win/loss findings.
6. **Management & Organization** — team assessment, succession risk, comp benchmarking, MIP design.
7. **Growth / Value-Creation Plan** — 3-5 specific levers, quantified, with a Day-1 owner per lever.
8. **Historical Financials** — revenue / EBITDA / margin trajectory.
9. **QofE Findings & EBITDA Bridge** — reported → adjusted → pro-forma → run-rate, with defensibility tags.
10. **Working Capital, Net Debt & Capex** — peg, debt-like items, maintenance vs. growth capex.
11. **Deal Terms & Structure** — purchase price, sources & uses, leverage, rollover, R&W structure.
12. **Returns Analysis** — LBO returns waterfall, sensitivities, returns attribution (EBITDA growth × multiple × debt paydown). Math shown.
13. **Risk Factors** — ranked by severity × likelihood with specific mitigants.
14. **The Eight-Question Answers** — original thesis claim / current reality / variance grade / evidence / confidence.
15. **Diligence Findings Summary** — top 8-12 findings across workstreams, rated High/Med/Low with mitigation.
16. **Recommendation & Decision Memo** — final bid, conditions, what would change the recommendation.
17. **Open-Question / Diligence Gap List.**
18. **Sign-off Lines** — deliberately blank for human IC signature.
19. **Appendices** — A: comps & precedents; B: LBO model output; C: workstream summaries (one page each); D: source/citation log.
