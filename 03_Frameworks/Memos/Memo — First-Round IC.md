# FIRST-ROUND IC MEMO (~15 pages cap)

Opens with the [[House Style]] header; closes with its footer and blank sign-off lines. Voice rules and killer-slide checklist: [[Voice & Killer Slides]].

For IOI submission approval. Lighter on diligence findings (none yet); heavier on thesis.

1. **Executive Summary** (1 page) — deal header, recommendation (first-round bid at $X-$Y range), 3-5 thesis pillars, 3 risks, conditions to advance.
2. **Situation Overview** (half page) — process dynamics (broad auction / limited / proprietary), why we can win, expected competitive set.
3. **Company Snapshot** (1-2 pages) — what they do, history, key facts, customer logos, named C-suite.
4. **Industry & Market** (1-2 pages) — TAM/SAM/SOM (top-down + bottom-up triangulation), growth, segmentation, competitive landscape.
5. **Preliminary Financial View** (1-2 pages) — revenue/EBITDA trajectory, gross margin, growth decomposition; flag QofE-pendings.
6. **Investment Thesis** (2-3 pages) — 3-5 pillars with quantified value-creation levers; the moat slide; the bull case.
7. **Risks & Mitigants** (1 page) — top 5-7 risks with severity × likelihood and explicit mitigation.
8. **Returns Sketch** (1 page) — base/bull/bear MOIC and IRR at modeled entry; sensitivity to entry × exit multiple. Math shown.
9. **Diligence Plan & Budget for Exclusivity** (half page).
10. **Recommendation** (quarter page) — bid range with structure assumptions.
11. **Appendix** — comp table, LBO sketch summary.
