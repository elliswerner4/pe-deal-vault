# Mode: First-Round IC

**Trigger.** The firm decides to bid first-round. IOI bid letter due in ~4 weeks.

**Deliverable.** First-Round IC Memo (~15 pages, structure: [[Memo — First-Round IC]]).

**Workflow.**
1. Re-read the CIM. Build an initial LBO sketch (entry/exit multiple, leverage, illustrative IRR at asking range) — show all assumptions and math.
2. Light commercial diligence — outside-in market study from public sources; note where expert calls (GLG / Tegus) would sharpen it.
3. Validate market growth claims via independent sources (industry reports, public comp benchmarks).
4. Build 3-5 thesis pillars with a quantified value-creation hypothesis per pillar.
5. Identify the 5-7 top risks; mitigation plan per risk.
6. Base/bull/bear LBO sketch with sensitivity to entry × exit multiple.
7. Diligence plan + budget for exclusivity (workstream-by-workstream advisor selection).
8. Recommend IOI bid range with structure assumptions.

**Output shape.** ~15 pages cap. Killer slides 1-5 (thesis, moat, base/bull/bear, EBITDA bridge first cut, comp table).
