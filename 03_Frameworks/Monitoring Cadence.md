# Monitoring Cadence

Recurring monitoring for portfolio companies (and watch-list targets). Design principle: **alert-driven daily/weekly, report-driven monthly/quarterly** — cadence matches how fast each variable actually moves. Cash moves daily; pipeline weekly; EBITDA closes monthly; valuation settles quarterly; leadership turns over semi-annually; exit readiness needs a year-on-year view.

| Cadence | Task | Output |
|---|---|---|
| Daily | News + competitor + regulator alert (alert-only) | One-paragraph alert when material; silence otherwise |
| Weekly (Mon) | Company intel scan | One-page briefing → [[Weekly Briefing Template]] |
| Monthly (1st) | Regulatory + sector trade-press scan | One-page sector brief |
| Quarterly (Jan/Apr/Jul/Oct) | Variance vs. IC base + comp/mark refresh | [[Memo — Variance Report]] |
| Semi-annual (Jun/Dec) | Leadership + comp scan | Update if material |
| Annual (Dec, escalating year 4+) | Exit-readiness assessment | Scorecard; semi-annual in year 4, quarterly year 5+ |

## Materiality rubric (what surfaces vs. silent-logs)

Alert only on: company-named news of customer loss / leadership change / funding / regulatory action / litigation / M&A / cyber incident; competitor M&A, >$25M funding, directly-competing launch; sector regulatory action touching reimbursement, licensure, antitrust, or privacy. **Skip** PR puffery, generic think-pieces, and stock moves without an underlying event. Cadence determines how often work runs; materiality determines how often anyone hears about it.

## Watch-list escalation

Upgrade a company to watch-list (tightened cadence) on any of: top-customer event · C-suite departure · concentration shift >300bps · competitive disruption · reimbursement/compliance change · cyber incident · covenant headroom <20% · >15% variance for 2+ consecutive quarters · strategic-buyer activity in the sector · comp-set valuation moving >2× in 8 weeks.

Watch-list overrides: post-close first 12 months → daily KPI checks + weekly variance; covenant stress → weekly 13-week cash review + daily liquidity alert; exit window (year 4+) → quarterly exit-readiness + buyer-activity scan.
