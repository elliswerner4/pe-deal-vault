# Sector Playbook — Healthcare Services
Provider services, MSO/PC structures, behavioral health, dental, PT, dermatology, ophthalmology, home health, hospice, infusion, specialty pharmacy, DSOs.

**Current environment.** DOJ recovered **$6.8B in FCA settlements in FY2025** (highest ever; $5.7B from healthcare). 1,297 qui tam suits filed FY2025. CMS V28 risk-adjustment fully operational Jan 1, 2026 (HCC count expanded 86 → 115). CY2026 Home Health PPS Final Rule: net **-1.3% ($220M cut)**. CY2026 HOPPS expanded site-neutral payments cutting $290M. California AB 1415 / SB 351 (effective Jan 1, 2026) requires 90-day OHCA notice on PE healthcare deals.

### KPIs the agent computes

1. **Payer mix concentration** — % revenue by payer category (Medicare, MA, Medicaid, commercial, self-pay). Yellow >30% single payer; red >50%. MA-heavy assets need separate V28 impact modeling.
2. **Risk-adjusted revenue capture** — RAF score, RAF trajectory, projected V28 revenue drop (CMS projected -3.12% on average MA risk scores).
3. **Provider productivity** — wRVUs/FTE, encounters/day, panel size per PCP, surgical case volume per OR-day.
4. **Same-store / same-clinic revenue growth** — split from acquired growth.
5. **Locum/contract-labor dependency** — % clinical hours covered by locums. Above ~15% is structural risk.
6. **MSO take-rate and PC residual margin** — MSO % of clinic-level revenue / EBITDA; PC economic residual.
7. **Days in A/R, denial rate, clean-claim rate** — RCM proxies.

### Red flags

- Comp tied to volume or value of referrals (Stark/AKS exposure)
- CPOM / MSO structural defects (low-strike share transfer mechanics, MSO control over hiring/firing)
- FCA exposure in coding patterns (E/M code spike post-acquisition, MA risk scores rising vs. peers)
- HIPAA / cybersecurity gaps (post-Change Healthcare baseline: MFA on admin, encryption at rest/transit)
- State CON exposure (35 states + DC enforce CON laws; review by service category)
- 340B disruption (13 states enacted 340B contract pharmacy access laws in 2025)
- FTC non-compete rule dead but state-level enforceability varies (CA, ND, OK, MN ban; AR, MT healthcare-specific bans 2025)

### Regulatory dimensions
CY2026 Home Health PPS Final Rule (CMS-1828-F) · CY2026 HOPPS Final Rule (CMS-1834-FC) · V28 MA risk-adjustment · CPOM/MSO state laws (CA AB 1415, OR HB 4130, MA H.5159, IN HB 1666, WA HB 1881) · Stark/AKS FMV doctrine · DSCSA full implementation (large 11/27/2025; small 2026).

### Recent benchmarks (2024-2025)
- Global healthcare PE deal value: **$190B in 2025** (record)
- Dental: 137 add-ons + 6 platform buyouts + 18 growth deals in 2024 (most active specialty)
- Dermatology & ophthalmology: busiest 2025 specialties
- Home infusion: 11 new platform deals in 2024; platforms ~20× EBITDA, tuck-ins low double digits
- Home health/hospice: ~520 of 749 agencies 2006-2024 → middle-market PE
