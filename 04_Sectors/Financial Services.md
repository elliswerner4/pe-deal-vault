# Sector Playbook — Financial Services
Insurance distribution, payments, wealth management, RIAs, asset management, specialty lending.

**Current environment.** Most consolidated PE sector; most regulated. Insurance distribution PE multiples **16.7× EV/EBITDA avg 2022-YTD 2025**, vs. 13.1× 2019-2021. Wealth management / RIA median 11× in 2024; PE-backed buyers 73% of wealth/insurance advisory M&A in 2025. Payments 4-6× revenue / 8-12× EBITDA.

### KPIs the agent computes

1. **Organic vs. acquired growth** (insurance, wealth) — same-agency / same-RIA growth split
2. **Revenue per producer / advisor; AUM/AUA per advisor; retention of senior producers**
3. **Client retention by tenure cohort** (wealth) — 95%+ benchmark; >5% attrition needs explanation
4. **Take-rate / spread economics** — payments interchange net; insurance commission + contingents
5. **Loss ratios, combined ratios** (carriers/MGAs); **delinquency, charge-off, vintage performance** (specialty lending)
6. **Regulatory capital ratios** (lenders); **float yield + duration** (insurance)
7. **Compliance / supervisory cost as % of revenue**

### Red flags

- Carrier/payer/lender concentration in single counterparty
- Producer non-competes weak or unenforceable in key states
- Contingent commissions inflating reported margin (may not recur)
- Vintage credit deterioration masked by growth (denominator effect on charge-off rates)
- State licensing gaps
- Custody / clearing concentration
- AML/BSA program weakness

### Regulatory dimensions
SEC Marketing Rule · RIA custody rule · FINRA supervision · State insurance regulators (NAIC) · CFPB rules · OCC/FDIC for bank-partner structures · Card-network operating rules.
