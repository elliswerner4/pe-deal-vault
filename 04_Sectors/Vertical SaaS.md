# Sector Playbook — Vertical SaaS
**Current environment** (sourced; verify before quoting at IC). Median private SaaS multiples have compressed materially from 2021 peaks; recent SEG / Aventis / SaaS Capital data point to ~4-5× ARR median in 2025, with premium vertical SaaS at 7-9× and the strongest retention/Rule-of-40 names commanding double-digit multiples (specific quoted figures like "11.7× for NRR>120%" should be re-verified against the cited source before use in an IC memo). Vertical SaaS does tend to trade at a meaningful premium to horizontal at comparable performance — the 25-30% figure is conventional wisdom and should be cited to Software Equity Group or Battery Ventures OpenCloud where used. 2,698 SaaS transactions in 2025 per SEG; 44% of 2024 SaaS deals vertical-focused.

### KPIs the agent computes

1. **ARR + Growth Rate** — quality-of-ARR (contracted vs. usage, multi-year vs. monthly). Median private B2B SaaS growth 25% (SaaS Capital 2025).
2. **Net Dollar Retention (NDR) / Gross Dollar Retention (GDR)** — NDR ≥120% premium; 100-120% healthy; <100% needs explanation. GDR <85% signals product/competitive weakness.
3. **Gross Logo Retention** — vertical SaaS should be 90%+; SMB-focused 80-85%.
4. **Rule of 40 / Rule of X** — growth + EBITDA margin ≥40. Each 10-pt improvement above threshold ≈ 1.0-1.5× ARR multiple. Bessemer's Rule of X correlates 62% with valuation vs. Rule of 40 at 50%.
5. **CAC Payback** — median 20 months (KeyBanc 2024). SMB target <12 months; enterprise tolerates 18-24.
6. **Magic Number** — Net New ARR / S&M Spend (lagged). Median 0.90; target ≥1.0; top quartile ≥2.0.
7. **Gross Margin** — >80% earned median 7.6× multiple; <80% earned 5.5× (SEG 2024).

### Red flags

- Aging architecture / single-tenant deployments with per-customer code branches
- Key-person engineering risk; no documentation
- Tech debt with deprecated dependencies (Windows Server 2012, MySQL 5.7, Python 2)
- Top-10 customer concentration >35% (vertical) or >50% (red)
- Pricing/packaging chaos; no published price book
- Inflated ARR definition (includes professional services or non-renewing pilots)
- Sales-rep ramp time blowing out; reps churning before reaching quota
- No SOC 2 Type II for B2B target serving regulated customers
- GPL/AGPL OSS in proprietary code

### Regulatory dimensions
Data residency (GDPR, CPRA, sector-specific) · AI/ML training-data exposure · State privacy patchwork (CA, CO, CT, UT, VA, TX active) · HIPAA for health-vertical SaaS · GLBA for FI-vertical.

### Comp sources
Bessemer Cloud 100 Benchmarks; BVP Nasdaq Emerging Cloud Index; KeyBanc Capital Markets / Sapphire Ventures Private SaaS Survey (16th annual 2024); Software Equity Group SaaS M&A Quarterly; OpenView Benchmarks; ChartMogul; SaaS Capital; Battery Ventures OpenCloud; Meritech Public Comps.

### Recent benchmarks
- Q1 2025: 73 PE-led enterprise SaaS deals (+66% over 2024)
- Vista Equity deployed $12.4B into vertical SaaS roll-ups through 2024
- Hg's $6.4B OneStream acquisition (Jan 2026) — flagship vertical SaaS comp
- Cloud 100 average revenue multiple = 20× (down from 34× in 2021)
