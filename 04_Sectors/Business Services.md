# Sector Playbook — Business Services
Route-based services, MSPs, staffing, facilities maintenance, accounting/HR outsourcing, marketing services, environmental services.

**Current environment.** Houlihan Lokey #1 global business services M&A advisor with 86 deals in 2024. PE add-on activity in home/business services rose **88% YoY through mid-2025** (Capstone).

### KPIs the agent computes

1. **Customer concentration** — top customer >10% potential flag; top 5 >25% flag; top customer >30% yellow; >50% often kills deal. Top customers by *GP%*, not revenue.
2. **Route density / stop density** (route-based: pest, HVAC, waste, uniform, food service) — stops/truck-hour, miles per stop, $/route. Target: 80%+ fleet utilization.
3. **Recurring revenue %** — subscription/contract revenue / total. >70% premium multiple; <40% project/discretionary.
4. **Billable utilization** (professional/staffing) — billable hours / available hours per FTE. Consulting/MSP target 70-80%; staffing 85%+.
5. **Gross margin per FTE / Revenue per FTE** — sector-dependent: MSPs $250-400K/FTE; accounting outsourcing $150-250K; staffing $300-500K.
6. **Contract tenure & renewal rate** — average length, % auto-renewing, % evergreen.
7. **Cost-per-route / stops per truck-hour** (route-based); **average daily fill / bill rate / spread** (staffing).

### Red flags

- Single-customer >20% + contract expiring within 18 months
- Time-and-materials masquerading as recurring revenue
- Driver/technician turnover above 40%/yr (destroys density)
- Aging ERP / scheduling stack (legacy on-prem dispatch)
- Margin compression hidden by mix-shift
- Above-market wages without above-market productivity
- Customer "logo retention" on company-friendly definition (excludes M&A churn)

### Regulatory dimensions
DOL worker classification (IC vs. employee) · State data-privacy for outsourcing platforms · DOT/CDL for fleet-heavy operators · Bonding/licensing by state (HVAC, electrical, pest applicator).
