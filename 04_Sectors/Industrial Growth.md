# Sector Playbook — Industrial Growth
Specialty chemicals, building products, food/agri-processing, industrial distribution.

**Current environment.** 2025 manufacturing multiples reset to ~6.5× (League Park; First Page Sage). Specialty chemicals PE multiples 12.3× (2025) vs. 13.8× (2024); strategics 10.3×. Cyclicality and Phase II ESA outcomes decisive.

### KPIs the agent computes

1. **End-market exposure** — % revenue by end-market (auto, aero, energy, construction, ag, infra). Cyclicality coefficient.
2. **Capex intensity** — maintenance vs. growth split; maintenance as % of revenue, % of EBITDA. **Maintenance ≈ depreciation as starting peg.**
3. **Inventory turns, DSO, DPO, Cash Conversion Cycle** — trended over 36 months (surfaces "build to sell").
4. **Customer concentration AND end-market concentration** — both matter independently.
5. **Raw material as % of COGS + hedging program**.
6. **Order book / backlog coverage** — months of forward revenue.
7. **Plant utilization & OEE** — Overall Equipment Effectiveness.

### Red flags

- Adverse Phase I findings triggering Phase II ESA (especially solvent/chrome/PFAS exposure)
- PFAS-handling history (CERCLA hazardous-substance designation April 2024)
- Underfunded pension / OPEB obligations (PBGC takeover, multi-employer withdrawal liability — often 5-10× annual contribution)
- Union contracts expiring within 24 months
- Customer concentration in cyclical end markets
- Recent working-capital build (inventory days extending pre-sale)
- Tariff/trade exposure (China-input dependence)
- Customer-funded tooling legally belonging to customer

### Regulatory dimensions
EPA RCRA / CERCLA / TSCA · State brownfield programs · PFAS (CERCLA designation April 2024) · OSHA process-safety management · Tariff / Section 232 / Section 301 · CDFA / FDA for food and ag · Multi-employer pension withdrawal liability.

### Comp sources
Capstone Partners; Seale & Associates; Valuation Research Corp; League Park; BCG Industrial Goods; McKinsey Advanced Industries; Kroll Manufacturing.

### Recent benchmarks
- Manufacturing reset to ~6.5× average 2025
- Specialty/performance materials PE 12.3× (2025) vs. 13.8× (2024); strategic 10.3×
- Industrial distribution multiples +0.4× YoY 2025
- Global median EV/EBITDA all-sector 2025: ~9.3×; PE-led US deals at 12.8× vs. corporate-led 9.9× (CLFI 2025)
