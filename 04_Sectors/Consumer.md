# Sector Playbook — Consumer
CPG, restaurants, multi-unit retail, consumer services.

**Current environment.** CPG growth single-digit, volume contracting (Circana: +2% $ but -1 to -2% volume in 2024-2025). DTC will rise from ~10% to 15% of revenue share by 2030 (PwC). Diligence shift from shelf-space to "moments of intent."

### KPIs the agent computes

1. **Repeat rate / customer cohort LTV** — DTC/subscription brands
2. **AOV / basket size / units per transaction**
3. **Channel mix** — DTC, Amazon, mass retail, club, wholesale, marketplaces. Concentration >40% any channel risky.
4. **Marketing efficiency** — blended CAC, payback, MER, ROAS, contribution margin per cohort
5. **Multi-unit** — AUV, SSS growth, four-wall EBITDA margin, payback per new unit, cohort year-2 vs. year-1
6. **Gross / contribution margin** — after variable shipping, returns, marketing
7. **Inventory weeks of supply, sell-through, markdown %**

### Red flags

- Single-platform dependence (Amazon >50%) — algorithmic + suspension risk
- Cohort decay (newer cohorts lower LTV / higher CAC)
- Owner add-backs that don't survive scrutiny ("founder marketing" or "discontinued SKUs")
- Inflation pass-through failure (gross margin compression while peers held)
- New-unit payback extending past 36 months (multi-unit)
- Real-estate exposure on long leases at above-market rent
- Influencer/social-platform algorithmic dependence

### Regulatory dimensions
FTC endorsement guides (influencer marketing) · Prop 65 (CA labeling) · FDA labeling and DSHEA supplements · State EPR packaging laws (CA, OR, CO, ME).
