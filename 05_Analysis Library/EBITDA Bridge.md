**Notes on defensibility tags:**
- **Defensible** — documented one-time items, completed cost actions, signed contracts at run-rate
- **Pushback** — recurring "one-times" (legal/M&A/restructuring 3+ years running), aggressive owner add-backs
- **Rejected** — synthetic synergies for unannounced add-ons, projected price increases not yet implemented, run-rate of partial-year savings extrapolated linearly

**Credit-market constraint:** Modern credit agreements cap EBITDA addbacks at 25% of unadjusted EBITDA with 24-month look-forward. Aggressive sponsor PF gets haircut 30-50% by lenders.
