# Working Capital Walk & Peg

Every filled-in number must carry a shown derivation or a citation — never bare.

**Methodology.** TTM monthly average of operating NWC, smoothed for seasonality. Calculated as:

`NWC = (AR + Inventory + Prepaids) − (AP + Accrued Liabilities + Other Current Liabilities)`

Excludes: cash, debt, debt-like items, deferred tax, deal-related accruals.

**True-ups:**
- At signing: Estimated NWC at close vs. Peg
- At ~60-120 days post-close: Actual NWC vs. Estimated

**Traps to flag:**
- Seasonality (use 12-month rolling avg or quarter-end matching)
- DPO blowing out pre-close (suggests cash drain post-close)
- AR >90 days reserved adequately
- Inventory obsolescence
- Customer deposits / deferred revenue — seller wants in NWC, buyer wants as debt-like
